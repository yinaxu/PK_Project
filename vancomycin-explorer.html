Teaching points

01

Population pharmacokinetic models estimate drug disposition from patient covariates — age, body size, renal function, sex, and other validated clinical characteristics depending on the underlying model. It's a reasonable starting estimate, but real patients deviate — sometimes substantially — from the population average.

02

Bayesian (MAP) estimation combines that population prior with an observed concentration, weighting each by how much it's trusted: a tighter prior or a noisier assay shifts the balance one way or the other.

03

The typical therapeutic target of AUC24:MIC 400–600 (assuming MIC = 1 mg/L, consistent with current vancomycin therapeutic monitoring guidelines) reflects that efficacy and nephrotoxicity both track total drug exposure over 24 hours, not any single concentration.

04

One concentration rarely pins down a patient's true clearance exactly — it narrows the estimate. A second concentration, especially at a different point in the dosing interval, narrows it further.

05

Concentrations are only interpretable if drawn at steady state (typically after 3–5 half-lives on a stable regimen). A concentration drawn during the distribution phase, shortly after infusion ends, reflects redistribution rather than elimination and may not be appropriate for this model.

06

Repeat concentrations are generally most informative after major dose adjustments or significant changes in renal function. Depending on the platform and clinical context, concentrations obtained before steady state may still be informative — this is one reason Bayesian platforms can be more flexible than fixed-sampling nomograms.

Single-parameter MAP update on clearance only — volume of distribution is intentionally held constant at the population estimate in this educational model, to isolate the effect of Bayesian updating on clearance — with a log-normal prior (30% CV) and 20% proportional residual error. Commercial Bayesian dosing platforms use validated population pharmacokinetic models, simultaneous estimation of multiple PK parameters, and extensive clinical validation; this educational application is intended to demonstrate Bayesian concepts rather than provide clinical dosing recommendations.
