Sources & Further Reading

Vancomycin AUC-guided dosing

Rybak MJ, Le J, Lodise TP, et al. Therapeutic monitoring of vancomycin for serious methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus infections: A revised consensus guideline and review by the American Society of Health-System Pharmacists, the Infectious Diseases Society of America, the Pediatric Infectious Diseases Society, and the Society of Infectious Diseases Pharmacists. Am J Health-Syst Pharm. 2020;77(11):835–864. Source of the AUC24:MIC 400–600 consensus target used in the Vancomycin Explorer.

Matzke GR, McGory RW, Halstenson CE, Keane WF. Pharmacokinetics of vancomycin in patients with various degrees of renal function. Antimicrob Agents Chemother. 1984;25(4):433–437. Source of the population clearance equation used for the population PK prior.

Extended-interval aminoglycoside dosing

Nicolau DP, Freeman CD, Belliveau PP, Nightingale CH, Ross JW, Quintiliani R. Experience with a once-daily aminoglycoside program administered to 2,184 adult patients. Antimicrob Agents Chemother. 1995;39(3):650–655. Original Hartford nomogram study. The Aminoglycoside Explorer reconstructs the underlying trough-target logic of this nomogram rather than reproducing its published chart.

Renal function estimation

Cockcroft DW, Gault MH. Prediction of creatinine clearance from serum creatinine. Nephron. 1976;16(1):31–41. Source of the creatinine clearance equation used across both tools.

Bayesian forecasting in pharmacokinetics

Sheiner LB, Beal SL, Rosenberg B, Marathe VV. Forecasting individual pharmacokinetics. Clin Pharmacol Ther. 1979;26(3):294–305. Foundational paper describing Bayesian (MAP) forecasting of individual PK parameters — the general approach the Bayesian Explorer illustrates in simplified form.
