Clinical & Mathematical References

The algorithms, target parameters, and kinetic models used in this educational lab are derived from published consensus guidelines and established pharmacokinetic literature.

1. Vancomycin Therapeutic Drug Monitoring & Bayesian Estimation

  • Consensus Guidelines (AUC-Based Dosing):
    Rybak MJ, Le J, Lodise TP, et al. Therapeutic monitoring of vancomycin for serious methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus infections: A revised consensus guideline and review by the ASHP, IDSA, PIDS, and SIDP. Am J Health-Syst Pharm. 2020;77(11):835–864. Key basis for: target AUC24/MIC ratio (400–600 mg·h/L) and endorsement of Bayesian MIPD software.
  • Bayesian Forecasting in Clinical Practice:
    Neely MN, Youn G, Kovacevic B, et al. Are traditional target vancomycin trough concentrations adequate for optimal therapeutic outcomes? Antimicrob Agents Chemother. 2014;58(1):309–316. Key basis for: maximum a posteriori (MAP) parameter updates using population priors and individual serum levels.

2. Extended-Interval Aminoglycoside Dosing

  • Hartford Nomogram:
    Nicolau DP, Freeman CD, Belliveau PP, et al. Experience with a once-daily aminoglycoside dosing regimen incorporating a true pharmacodynamic endpoint. Antimicrob Agents Chemother. 1995;39(3):650–655. Key basis for: 7 mg/kg gentamicin/tobramycin dosing curves and interval-selection decision trees.

3. Basic Pharmacokinetics & Renal Function Estimation

  • Cockcroft-Gault Creatinine Clearance:
    Cockcroft DW, Gault MH. Prediction of creatinine clearance from serum creatinine. Nephron. 1976;16(1):31–41. Key basis for: baseline renal function estimation and elimination rate constant (Ke) derivations.
  • Clinical Pharmacokinetics Principles:
    Bauer LA. Applied Clinical Pharmacokinetics. 3rd ed. McGraw-Hill Education; 2014. Key basis for: first-order elimination equations, steady-state concentration estimates, and volume-of-distribution calculations.
