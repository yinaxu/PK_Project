About the project & creator

I am a pharmacist interested in how technology, software, and artificial intelligence can transform clinical workflows, therapeutic decision-making, and healthcare education.

Project Overview

Modern therapeutic drug monitoring is rapidly shifting toward Model-Informed Precision Dosing (MIPD) and software-guided decision support. However, many clinical tools operate as “black boxes,” obscuring the mathematical transitions between population models and patient-specific dosing.

I built the Clinical Pharmacokinetics Learning Lab to explore how interactive web tools can make complex pharmacokinetics—from Bayesian parameter estimation to Hartford nomogram interval selection—more visual, transparent, and intuitive.

What This Portfolio Demonstrates

  • Clinical Translation: Translating clinical guidelines (such as ASHP/IDSA vancomycin consensus guidelines and Hartford nomograms) into structured digital interfaces.
  • Tech & AI Aptitude: Utilizing modern web technologies and AI-assisted tools to prototype functional, interactive healthcare solutions.
  • Product & Visual Sense: Designing user-centered interfaces that break down multi-step calculations into clear visual insights.
  • Adaptability & Rapid Learning: Independently diving into health-system concepts, pharmacokinetics modeling, and modern tools to bridge clinical knowledge with digital delivery.
