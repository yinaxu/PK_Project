Not for clinical use

Every tool in this project is an educational simplification, not validated clinical software. It must not be used to make, guide, or verify a real dosing decision for a real patient.

Specifically:

  • Both tools use population-average or single-parameter simplified models. Validated clinical dosing software (including InsightRX Nova/Apollo and similar platforms) uses richer covariate models, multi-parameter Bayesian posteriors, and validated, peer-reviewed priors.
  • The aminoglycoside boundary curves are derived from the same target-trough logic that underlies the published Hartford nomogram, not digitized from its original chart — they approximate, but do not reproduce, the validated tool.
  • Neither tool accounts for drug interactions, organ dysfunction beyond renal function, obesity-adjusted dosing weights, pregnancy, dialysis, or other important clinical covariates.
  • No tool on this site stores, transmits, or is intended to receive real patient data.

These projects exist to make pharmacokinetic reasoning visible and learnable — for that, use them freely. For actual patient care, use your institution's validated dosing software, consult a clinical pharmacist, and follow current guidelines.
