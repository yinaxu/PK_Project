2. Mathematical & Model Limitations

The calculators on this platform utilize simplified single-compartment or basic pharmacokinetic mathematical models for conceptual clarity:

  • Gentamicin / Tobramycin: Interval predictions use derived single-dose kinetic boundaries designed to illustrate Hartford nomogram principles, rather than dynamic population-based Bayesian estimates.
  • Vancomycin: Dynamic simulations simplify volume of distribution (Vd) and elimination constants (Ke) to demonstrate steady-state concept curves rather than multi-compartment physiological realities.

Individual pharmacokinetic responses vary significantly based on patient-specific physiological factors, acute changes in renal function, fluid shifts, and co-administered medications.
